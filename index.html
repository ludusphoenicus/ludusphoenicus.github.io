Ludus Phoenicus

Ludus Phoenicus is heavily inspired by Tic-Tac-Toe. Specifically
an older variation of the game called "Terni Lapilli" where you
move the pieces in order to get 3 pieces in a single line row
whether it be straight or diagonal.

Each player has a limited amount of 4 pieces to place on the board.
After each player has placed down all of their 4 pieces, each player
must move a single piece that belongs to them on the board. The goal
of the game is to have 4 pieces aligned whether straight or diagona-
lly. A player may not place or move a piece to a square that already
has a piece on it. A player may not move a piece back to the square
they were already on after moving to another square 3-4 times in a
row.
